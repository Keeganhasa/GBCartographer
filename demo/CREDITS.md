# Demo project credits

A sample GB Studio 4 project for trying GB Cartographer. Every picture in it was made by the people below and
is used under the license they chose. Nothing here was generated by a machine. The only edits GB Cartographer's
author made: file names, the GB Studio sidecar files that describe each picture, and a color normalization on
four sprites (pure black `#000000`, and one brown `#583008` in the trout, became GB Studio's dark green `#071821`,
which is how GB Studio reads them anyway).

## Palettes

The `DWC-` palettes in `project/palettes/` are GB Cartographer's author's own, made for the game Chorbi (the
Dustwatch Center set, with day / sunset / night variants). The `WIN-` palettes were made for the winter scene
here, and `WIN-SP1-Folk` for its sprites. All CC0.
